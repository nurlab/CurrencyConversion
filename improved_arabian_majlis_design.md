# Improved Arabian Majlis Design Specifications

## Room Layout & Dimensions
- **Dimensions**: 6 meters by 4 meters (24 square meters)
- **Shape**: L-shaped seating configuration
- **Removed elements**: Right side seating section, central coffee table

## Majlis Seating Design
### Configuration
- **Layout**: L-shaped arrangement along two walls (back wall and left wall)
- **Removed**: Right side seating section completely eliminated
- **Seating sections**: 
  - Back wall: 6-meter long section
  - Left wall: 4-meter long section

### Color Scheme
- **Primary upholstery**: Light gray fabric
- **Accent colors**: White
- **Pillows**: Mix of light gray and white throw pillows
- **Bolster pillows**: Light gray with white geometric patterns

### Design Elements
- **Backrests**: Vertically tufted in light gray
- **Seat cushions**: Flat, comfortable light gray cushions
- **Base frieze**: Dark wood or metallic base with intricate geometric star/octagon pattern in gold or silver
- **Throw pillows**: Square and cylindrical shapes in light gray and white
- **Corner placement**: Bolster pillows at L-shaped corner junction

## Flooring & Carpet
### Carpet Design
- **Style**: Traditional Arabian carpet pattern
- **Colors**: Light blue and white
- **Pattern**: Intricate geometric motifs typical of Arabian design
- **Coverage**: Fills the entire L-shaped seating area
- **Size**: Custom fit for 6m x 4m room

## Room Features
### Walls & Windows
- **Wall color**: Light cream or off-white
- **Molding**: White crown molding and baseboards
- **Windows**: Sheer white curtains with white roller blinds
- **Lighting**: Ambient lighting throughout

### Removed Elements
- **Central coffee table**: Completely removed
- **Right side seating**: Eliminated to create L-shape
- **Table accessories**: All table-related elements removed

## Design Philosophy
This improved design creates a more spacious, elegant Arabian Majlis with:
- Clean L-shaped configuration for better conversation flow
- Light gray and white color scheme for modern elegance
- Traditional Arabian carpet in light blue and white
- Generous 6m x 4m dimensions for comfortable seating
- Focus on the seating area without table distractions
